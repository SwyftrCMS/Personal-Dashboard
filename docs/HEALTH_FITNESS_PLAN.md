# Daily Health & Fitness Plan

## Starting point

Male, 31 years, 175 cm, 90 kg. Office-based work. Initial goal: 85 kg. Estimated starting intake: approximately 2,000 kcal/day, 130--150 g protein. Aim for gradual loss of 0.25--0.5 kg/week; adjust after 3--4 weeks based on progress and recovery.

## 4. Night stretching --- 10 minutes

After a workout or gentle warm-up. Each minute includes changing sides where applicable. Hold gently, breathe normally, never bounce or force a toe touch.

  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Time                    Stretch                 Instructions
  ----------------------- ----------------------- ---------------------------------------------------------------------
  1 min                   Calves                  Wall stretch, 30 sec each side

  1 min                   Hamstrings              Lie on back, hold behind thigh, gently straighten knee; 30 sec each

  1 min                   Hip flexors             Half-kneeling or standing split stance, 30 sec each

  1 min                   Quadriceps              Supported standing heel towards buttock, 30 sec each

  1 min                   Glutes                  Lying figure-four, 30 sec each

  1 min                   Inner thighs            Seated butterfly, knees relaxed

  1 min                   Upper-back rotation     Open book, 30 sec each side

  1 min                   Chest                   Gentle doorway stretch, two short holds

  1 min                   Back and shoulders      Comfortable child's pose or supported forward reach

  1 min                   Relaxation              Lie down, slow breathing
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Stretch to mild tension only. Stop if sharp pain, tingling or numbness occurs. On very sore days reduce range or substitute easy walking.

## 5. Weekly exercise schedule

  Day         Activity
  ----------- --------------------------------------------------
  Monday      20-minute home workout
  Tuesday     20--30-minute easy walk
  Wednesday   Oztag, 40-minute game plus 10--15-minute warm-up
  Thursday    10--20-minute recovery walk if comfortable
  Friday      Rest or 20-minute easy walk
  Saturday    20-minute home workout if recovered
  Sunday      30-minute easy walk

Walk at a conversational pace. Start near 6,000 daily steps if comfortable, gradually increasing towards 8,000. Walk at lunch or earlier in the evening when possible; indoor walking is fine if you cannot leave the kids unattended.

### Quiet home workout --- 20 minutes

No weights, jumping or gym. Use a sturdy chair or wall if needed.

Warm up for 3 minutes: marching, arm circles, gentle hip hinges.

  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Exercise                 Repetitions             Technique
  ------------------------ ----------------------- -------------------------------------------------
  Chair squats             8--12                   Sit back towards chair, stand slowly

  Wall or bench push-ups   8--12                   Keep body straight

  Glute bridges            10--12                  Lie on back, knees bent, lift hips

  Standing calf raises     10--15                  Hold wall for balance

  Dead bugs                6 each side             Lie on back, slowly extend opposite arm and leg

  Plank                    15--25 sec              Knees down if needed; breathe normally
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Complete 1--2 rounds within 15 minutes, resting 30--60 seconds as needed. Finish with 2 minutes of easy movement, then night stretching. Keep effort moderate; finish each set with several repetitions still possible. Dead bugs and planks replace sit-ups initially; they train the core without needing repeated spinal flexion.

### Four-week progression

-   Week 1: One round per session, easy effort.
-   Week 2: One or two rounds if soreness has settled.
-   Week 3: Two rounds, adding 1--2 repetitions where comfortable.
-   Week 4: Maintain two rounds; add a few seconds to planks or use a slightly lower push-up surface if ready.
-   Do not progress if still sore from Oztag. Skip or reduce a session rather than making it up later.

### Five-minute fallback

1 minute easy marching, then 1 minute each of chair squats, wall push-ups, glute bridges and dead bugs, with rests as needed. Skip entirely if exhausted or bedtime is at risk.

## 7. Wednesday Oztag

Game starts between 7:00 and 9:30 pm. No additional workout. - Eat usual dinner 2--3 hours before the game where possible. If needed, split dinner into a smaller pre-game portion and the remainder afterwards. Move the banana to 30--60 minutes before play if helpful. - Warm up 10--15 minutes: easy jogging, leg swings, walking lunges, lateral shuffles and progressively faster accelerations. Avoid long static holds immediately before sprinting. - After play, walk gently, hydrate and eat any remaining dinner. Skip the full night stretch if late; get to bed as soon as practical. A 9:30 pm start necessarily overrides the usual bedtime. - Thursday is recovery only; no hard training.

## 8. Tracking and health

-   Weigh yourself 2--3 mornings weekly under similar conditions and compare weekly averages.
-   Measure waist and comfortable fingertip-to-floor distance every 2 weeks; do not force the stretch.
-   If weight is unchanged after 3--4 weeks of consistent habits, reassess portions and activity. If losing rapidly or performance/recovery worsens, increase intake and seek advice.
-   Persistent morning leg soreness and fatigue lasting most of the week warrant a GP and sports physiotherapist assessment before increasing exercise. Ask about blood pressure, blood glucose and cholesterol given abdominal weight distribution.
-   Seek urgent care for severe muscle pain or weakness with dark urine after exercise, or a suddenly swollen, painful leg.
